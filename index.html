Build Process

Practical, iterative, and grounded in whether the object can survive real players using it in the wild.

01

Frame the experience

Define what the player should notice, infer, manipulate, and feel before choosing the mechanism.

02

Prototype the interaction

Test sensing, tolerances, feedback, and failure cases early with low-friction hardware.

03

Engineer the enclosure

Turn the interaction into a durable object with service access, cable management, and repeatable assembly.

04

Tune for humans

Playtest timing, affordances, reliability, and puzzle clarity until the build feels intentional.
