Adventure Camp

A three-day immersive gathering in the Mendocino redwoods, staged by The Explorers Guild — puppets, puzzles, and songs sending teams of five on a hero’s journey to protect a world of magical creatures. Built by a small core team I was part of, from concept through production. Winner of No Proscenium’s Best Show of 2025.

Photo: Mayu Shimizu

The Solano Human Project

A self-authored public ARG staged across several blocks of Solano Avenue in Albany, CA — a hybrid of street quest, escape room, and puzzle hunt where teams thwart a rogue AI through SMS facilitation, dead drops, and puzzles hidden in real storefronts. Reviewed by Room Escape Artist, and nominated for a TERPECA award and a No Proscenium Audience Award (an “Immie”).

History in Bloom

A commissioned, ticketed location-based immersive experience for the Conservatory of Flowers and KQED, built with a four-person team under Urban Replay. Blends archival research and historical timelines with physical puzzle mechanics for a seamless, self-guided narrative hunt.

LEXACUBE

A custom-engineered word game of six smart cubes (up to twelve in two-player mode), using unipolar analog Hall effect sensors and neodymium magnets for error-tolerant grid identification. Winner of an Editors’ Choice award at Bay Area Maker Faire 2025, and played at Burning Man, unSCruz, Neotropolis (shown here), and El Cerrito Preschool Cooperative’s “ECPC After Dark” fundraiser.

Eine Kleine Knock Music

A multiplayer rhythm game: players knock in time with a 300-LED chase strip that streams the beat down a line of stations. Custom-encoded songs scatter beats across the stations for coordinated call-and-response, scored on timing accuracy — all driven by a Raspberry Pi and piezo-sensing ESP32 nodes on an RS-485 bus. Shown here in play at the SF Pursuit scavenger hunt, where over 800 people played it across a single weekend.

Large-Format LED Display

A large-format LED matrix display I designed and built, mounted on a Burning Man art car — shown here running a Wordle-style word game for the crowd, and also the canvas for LEXACUBE and other word games on the playa.
